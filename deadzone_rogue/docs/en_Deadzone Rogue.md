# Deadzone Rogue

## What does randomization do to this game?

The unlocks you normally earn by completing missions are shuffled into the multiworld. Completing missions
sends checks out to other players instead. What you receive:

- **Weapons, perk families and augments**, one item each. By default, the ones that are normally free from the
  start are shuffled too. The client locks them until you receive them, and you begin with a small random
  starting kit so the first runs are playable.
- **Item Packs, Mastery Packs and Superior Item Packs**: the run items, Tech upgrades and SuperItem upgrades,
  dealt out in random packs (pack size is an option).
- **Unlocks**: the "Fate" rerolls, Fabricator restocks, Tier 10 Tech Upgrades and Epic Beginnings.
- **Zone Access** (optional), which unlocks zones 2–4. Difficulties are never locked; each one is just a separate check.

## What counts as a location check?

- Completing each mission on each difficulty you enable, as separate checks, for example
  "Who am I? (Adventure)", "Who am I? (Normal)", "Who am I? (Hard)". Story and challenge missions both count.
- Optionally, every 5 rooms cleared across all your runs ("Rooms Cleared: 5", "Rooms Cleared: 10", ...).

## What is the goal?

Complete "Getting Answers" (the final story mission) or, for a shorter game, "Get Out of My Head". Either one
counts on any difficulty.

## DeathLink

When another player dies, your current run isn't ended by default. Instead you lose a random perk, depending on
`death_link_mode`. Other modes are losing an item, losing Scrap, taking heavy damage, being pushed back a room
(experimental), or a real death.

## What does another world's item look like in Deadzone Rogue?

A notification appears on screen naming the item and the player it was sent to.
